Field weather notes

the meteogram in words

Day by day wind, snow, cloud, temperature, pressure for each day
AMPS against the station reports
    AMPS at Syowa's grid point, as shown on this page at the time Syowa report Molodezhnaya report (near E1)

    Temperature

    Red: 2 m air temperature, daily highs and lows. Blue dashes: wind chill. Symbols every 6 h: clear, cloud or snow.

    Cloud and snow

    Grey: likely cloud by altitude (air saturated over ice; right scale). Orange ticks: cloud base. Blue bars: snow, mm of water per 3 h (about 1 cm of fresh snow per mm).

    Wind aloft

    850, 700 and 500 hPa every 6 h: arrow for direction, number for knots. Heights are the model’s.

    Wind

    10 m wind in m/s (left) and knots (right). Arrows show where it blows to; letters, where it comes from. Dashed: strong (10 m/s) and gale (15 m/s). AMPS has no gusts.

    Field weather notes

    Amber: drifting snow (20 kt; 14 kt on fresh snow), snow of 0.5 mm or more, thick low cloud, or a fast pressure fall. Red: blowing snow (35 kt) or whiteout risk. “Handbook” lines come from the site’s nearest station section. “Last run”: a hazard in two runs in a row is more reliable.

    AMPS against the station reports

    AMPS at Syowa compared with the Syowa reports (Molodezhnaya shown too). A steady bias tells you how to read the charts, e.g. nights 2 °C too cold.

    Pressure

    Sea-level pressure. Red: a fall of 4 hPa or more in 12 h, often the first sign of a storm.

    How this page works where the numbers come from
    1. 🌍

      1. The model: AMPS

      NCAR’s weather model tuned for Antarctica: 8 km grid, twice a day, 5 days ahead.

      runs at 00 and 12 UTC, online about 10 h later
    2. ⏱

      2. An automatic job collects it

      A GitHub job checks every 30 minutes while a run is due and downloads only the values at the grid points nearest the seven sites (a few hundred kB).

      09:00–12:00 and 21:00–00:30 UTC
    3. 📈

      3. Charts and field notes

      It draws the charts and writes the notes by fixed handbook rules, comparing each day with the previous run. Every rule and its source ↗

    4. 🔍

      4. A check against real weather

      It compares AMPS with the Syowa and Molodezhnaya station reports (via Ogimet).

    5. 📱

      5. This page

      The last 8 runs (4 days). Each site also links to meteoblue for a second opinion.

    Data: AMPS (NCAR, for NSF), nearest 8 km grid point.
